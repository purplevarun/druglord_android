# Runs end by survival, not by a day limit

Saudagar descends from Drug Lord, where a fixed day limit is the core clock — so its absence here
needs saying out loud. We chose living costs plus a ten-day collector clock instead: your wallet
is the clock, "Day N" counts days survived, and a run ends only by retiring, going broke, or
meeting the collector unpaid. The day limit was rejected because it made the ending arithmetic
(finish before day N) instead of survival arithmetic (stay solvent), and it made "one more day"
a free action — here every day spent is a day of keep billed. The cost: interest and rent must
tick only on days that actually pass, or debt compounds per tap.
